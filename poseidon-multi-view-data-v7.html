Why are we losing customers in the North region?

The issue is not primarily Sales. Most recent churn in the North is concentrated in customers that experienced delayed deliveries after a supplier substitution.

What Poseidon found

Delivery delays explain most of the churn increase 8 of the last 11 lost customers had at least one late delivery in the 60 days before cancellation.
The problem started after a supplier substitution NorthCare temporarily replaced DentalPro for several high-volume products. Average lead time rose from 4.2 to 8.7 working days.
Sales discounting is a symptom, not the cause Account managers increased discounts to retain affected customers, which reduced margin without solving the service problem.

What I would do next

Protect customers before they experience the delay Use supplier lead time + stock forecast + customer commitments to identify risk before an SLA is missed.
Give Sales recovery actions, not generic alerts For each affected account, show the expected delay, commercial value, renewal timing and safest recovery option.

Evidence used

11 churned North-region customersCustomer history, cancellations and renewal outcomes.
42 delayed deliveriesOrders, fulfilment dates and support complaints.
Supplier lead-time historyDentalPro and NorthCare performance during the affected period.
Sales activityDiscounts, retention attempts and account notes.
